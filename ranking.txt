================================================
  RHYTHIANS RANKED SYSTEM — FULL REFERENCE
================================================

Rhythians uses a competitive ladder built around Rhythian Points (RHP).
There are 9 ranks, each split into 5 tiers. You climb by beating maps and
daily maps; you can also lose RHP by failing challenge maps, so the ladder
stays fair and everyone plays maps near their own skill level.

------------------------------------------------
1. THE RANKS
------------------------------------------------
Each rank spans 500 RHP and contains 5 tiers (100 RHP each).
Your rank is based purely on your current RHP.

  RANK      RHP RANGE        MAP RATING RANGE   COLOR
  Copper    0     - 500      0.00 - 1.49        Copper
  Bronze    501   - 1000     1.50 - 1.99        Bronze
  Silver    1001  - 1500     2.00 - 2.49        Silver
  Gold      1501  - 2000     2.50 - 2.99        Gold
  Platinum  2001  - 2500     3.00 - 3.49        Platinum
  Emerald   2501  - 3000     3.50 - 3.99        Emerald
  Diamond   3001  - 3500     4.00 - 4.49        Diamond
  Master    3501  - 4000     4.50 - 4.99        Master
  Expert    4001+            5.00 - 9.99        Expert

Each of the first 8 ranks has 5 tiers:
  Tier 1  = first 100 RHP of the rank
  Tier 2  = second 100 RHP
  Tier 3  = third 100 RHP
  Tier 4  = fourth 100 RHP
  Tier 5  = fifth 100 RHP (the top tier, next stop is the next rank)

Examples:
  Copper 1  = 0 - 100 RHP
  Copper 2  = 101 - 200 RHP
  Copper 3  = 201 - 300 RHP
  Copper 4  = 301 - 400 RHP
  Copper 5  = 401 - 500 RHP
  Bronze 1  = 501 - 600 RHP   (just crossed into Bronze)
  ...and so on up to Master 5 = 3501 - 4000 RHP.

The Expert rank has no tiers. Instead, it shows your GLOBAL position
(#1, #2, #3...) on the rank badge. It is the peak of the ladder.

------------------------------------------------
2. RHYTHIAN POINTS (RHP)
------------------------------------------------
RHP is the currency of the ranked ladder. Everyone starts at 0 RHP
(Copper 1). You earn RHP by completing content, and you can lose RHP
by failing a challenge map.

How you GAIN RHP:
  - Challenge maps: beating any map inside your rank's rating range
      awards base RHP based on YOUR RANK (higher ranks earn slightly less,
      keeping the ladder balanced), scaled by accuracy and speed.
  - Daily maps: the daily map uses the exact same rank-based system.

  Base RHP per rank (at 100% accuracy, no modifiers):
    Copper   -> 20 RHP
    Bronze   -> 19 RHP
    Silver   -> 18 RHP
    Gold     -> 17 RHP
    Platinum -> 16 RHP
    Emerald  -> 15 RHP
    Diamond  -> 14 RHP
    Master   -> 13 RHP
    Expert   -> 10 RHP

  So a 1.00 map in Copper and a 4.70 map in Master are each worth that
  rank's base, scaled by how you played it.

How you LOSE RHP:
  - Failing a challenge map (a failed pass recorded on your linked
      Rhythia profile for that map) costs you RHP based on:
        * how many people in total have beaten the map, and
        * what place your failed attempt would have earned.
      The better your attempt (higher placement), the less you lose.
      An attempt with no recorded accuracy counts as last place and
      takes the full loss. See section 5.

RHP never goes below 0, and a failed map only counts once per map
(the first recorded attempt).

------------------------------------------------
3. HOW CHALLENGE MAP RATING WORKS
------------------------------------------------
Every map on the site has a rating with two decimal places (e.g. 2.05).
This rating decides WHICH RANKS are allowed to play the map for points.

A typical 5-star Rhythia map is around a 2.05 rating. The conversion
from Rhythia stars to our rating is:

    Rhythia rating = stars x 0.41  (rounded to 2 decimals)

Each rank may ONLY earn RHP from maps inside its rating band:
  Copper   -> maps rated 0.00 - 1.49
  Bronze   -> maps rated 1.50 - 1.99
  Silver   -> maps rated 2.00 - 2.49
  Gold     -> maps rated 2.50 - 2.99
  Platinum -> maps rated 3.00 - 3.49
  Emerald  -> maps rated 3.50 - 3.99
  Diamond  -> maps rated 4.00 - 4.49
  Master   -> maps rated 4.50 - 4.99
  Expert   -> maps rated 5.00 - 9.99

This keeps everyone playing maps at their skill level. A Copper player
cannot gain points from a Diamond map, and a Diamond player cannot gain
points from a Bronze map. If you play a map outside your range, you earn
NOTHING (and it is not submitted for points).

WHO SETS THE RATING:
  - Submitters suggest a rating when they submit a map.
  - The map-reviewer confirms (or overrides) the final rating when they
    approve the map. The reviewer's rating is what actually counts.

------------------------------------------------
4. RHP GAIN — ACCURACY & SPEED
------------------------------------------------
The base RHP for a challenge map or daily map depends on YOUR RANK:

    Copper   -> 20 RHP   (at 100% accuracy, no modifiers)
    Bronze   -> 19 RHP
    Silver   -> 18 RHP
    Gold     -> 17 RHP
    Platinum -> 16 RHP
    Emerald  -> 15 RHP
    Diamond  -> 14 RHP
    Master   -> 13 RHP
    Expert   -> 10 RHP

It does NOT scale with the map's rating — a map at the bottom of your
range is worth the same as a map at the top. Higher ranks earn slightly
less so the ladder stays balanced at every level.

Your accuracy scales the base:
  Accuracy  >= 100%  -> 100% of base RHP
  Accuracy  >=  99%  ->  90% of base RHP
  Accuracy  >=  98%  ->  75% of base RHP
  Accuracy  >=  95%  ->  60% of base RHP
  Accuracy  >=  90%  ->  50% of base RHP
  Accuracy  <   90%  ->  40% of base RHP

Example (Copper, base 20 RHP):
  100% accuracy  -> 20 RHP
   99% accuracy  -> 18 RHP
   98% accuracy  -> 15 RHP
   95% accuracy  -> 12 RHP
  <90% accuracy  ->  8 RHP

Playing with a SPEED MODIFIER (anything above 1.00x) adds a bonus:
  +25% of the base per 1.00x of extra speed, capped at +50%.
  A Copper 2.00x perfect clear earns 20 x 1.25 = 25 RHP.
  A Master 2.00x perfect clear earns 13 x 1.25 = 16 RHP.
  A 3.00x+ clear earns up to 1.5x the base.

------------------------------------------------
5. RHP LOSS — PLACEMENT BASED
------------------------------------------------
Failing a challenge map costs RHP. The loss is based on two things:
  - totalBeaters: how many players have beaten the map, and
  - yourPlace:    the placement your failed attempt would have earned
                  among those beaters (by accuracy, 1 = best).

    loss = baseLoss x (yourPlace - 1) / totalBeaters
    baseLoss = round(rating x 10)   (minimum 5 RHP)

  - If you are effectively #1 on the map (your failed accuracy beats
    every existing score), you lose nothing.
  - If your attempt is average, you lose a portion of the base loss.
  - If no one has beaten the map, or your attempt has no recorded
    accuracy, you are treated as last place and take the full loss.

Example: a 2.05-rated map (base loss = 21). If 10 players have beaten
it and your failed attempt would place 5th, you lose 21 x 4/10 = 8 RHP.
If it would place last, you lose the full 21 RHP.

------------------------------------------------
6. THE DAILY MAP
------------------------------------------------
Every day at midnight UTC a new daily map is picked FOR EACH RANK. The
daily map is chosen at random from maps inside that rank's rating range,
so it is always matched to your skill level.

  - Beating the daily map awards the SAME rank-based RHP as challenge
    maps (your rank's base at 100% accuracy), scaled by accuracy and
    speed. Copper 20 RHP ... Expert 10 RHP.
  - Each rank gets its own daily map (Copper players see a Copper map,
    Bronze players see a Bronze map, etc.).
  - Maps are not repeated within a calendar month, then the pool resets.

STREAKS:
  Beating the daily map on consecutive days builds a STREAK.
  - If you beat the map yesterday too, your streak increases by 1.
  - If you miss a day, your streak resets to 0.
  - The daily leaderboard ranks players by their streak, within their
    own rank only.

------------------------------------------------
7. THE LEADERBOARDS
------------------------------------------------
There are two kinds of leaderboard, each split per-rank (a separate
leaderboard for Copper, Bronze, Silver, etc.):

  DAILY LEADERBOARDS:
    Ranks players by their daily map STREAK within their rank.
    A Bronze player with a 50-day streak ranks above a Master with a
    30-day streak ON THE BRONZE BOARD. Cross-rank comparison does not
    matter — each leaderboard is only about the players in that rank.

  CHALLENGE LEADERBOARDS:
    Ranks players by their RHP within their rank. Everyone starts at the
    bottom of their rank's board and climbs by earning RHP.

Only Rhythia-VERIFIED members appear on (or contribute to) the
leaderboards. Unverified accounts are never listed, and their RHP does
not count toward anyone's global placement.

Because the ladder goes both up and down, players who stop playing or
stop progressing settle into the lower ranks instead of hoarding points.
