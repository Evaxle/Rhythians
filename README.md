# Rhythians

Rhythians is a community site built around Rhythia. It gives players one place to track their progress, compete with other players, find maps, and take part in community events.

## What Rhythians does

### Rankings

Players can link their Rhythia profile and track their RHP across Lock, Spin, and VR. Rankings are used throughout the site to show player progress and help organize competitive features.

### Challenges

Challenges give players maps to complete outside of normal ranked play. Players can work through maps at their level, earn progress, and move into harder challenges as they improve.

### Battles

Battles are head-to-head matches between players. Rhythians handles matchmaking, map selection, match progress, and battle rankings. Battle Rank and RBP run in seasons so players have a fresh competitive ladder to climb.

### Tournaments

Tournaments are organized competitive events with different formats and player splits. Players can sign up, receive tournament maps, play their matches, and move through the bracket as the event progresses.

### Maps

The map sections help players find maps for different camera modes and skill levels. Rhythians also uses maps throughout challenges, battles, daily play, and tournaments.

### Profiles and community

Profiles bring a player's Rhythia progress and community activity together. Players can also share clips, connect with other players, and link Twitch or TikTok to their profile.

## Development

Rhythians is built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Prisma, and Supabase.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```
